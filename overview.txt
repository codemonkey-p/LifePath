 LifePath is a voice-first companion app for seniors with Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, or memory loss. Instead of typing, users just talk. When you first open LifePath, a talking guide sets up your profile, including your medications, family, doctor, emergency contact etc. Then an AI voice companion checks in every day, gives reminders for medications, appointments, and birthdays when they're due, and adds new reminders or shopping list items when you ask. You can also chat with it anytime about how you're feeling. LifePath includes brain games, like a voice quiz, a family quiz, and tap-only memory games. The goal is to help seniors stay independent and connected, and to give their families peace of mind.
